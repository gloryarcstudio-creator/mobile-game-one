The studio

Small team, big moon

Protek Games is a small independent studio. We design our games around short sessions, generous progress and characters you want to see again tomorrow.

Most ads in our games are optional rewards. Progress is never locked behind ads, players who buy anything stop seeing interstitials, and every game ships in many languages from day one.

Fair to players

You can finish our games without paying. Purchases speed things up or add style, they never lock the story.

Made for phones

One-thumb controls, portrait screens, offline progress and saves that stay on your device.

Built to last

Seasonal events, new districts and new heroes keep arriving after launch.
