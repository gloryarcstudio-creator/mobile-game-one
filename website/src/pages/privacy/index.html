Who we are

Protek Games ("we", "us") is an independent developer of mobile games operated by PROTEK s.r.o., which is responsible for the data described here. This policy explains what information our games and this website collect, why, who receives it, and what you can do about it. If you have any question, write to info@protekgames.com.

Short version

  • Our games have no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number, and your progress is saved on your own device.
  • Free games show ads. Ad companies may use your device's advertising ID to choose and measure ads. In Europe and the UK we ask for consent first, and on iPhone we ask for tracking permission.
  • We use analytics and crash reports to see how the game is played and to fix bugs. These use technical identifiers, not your name.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Data in our games

Game progress. Levels, currencies, purchased items and settings are stored locally on your device. We have no game servers, so this data is not uploaded to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising. Our free games show rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch for a bonus, and interstitial ads between play sessions. Players who have made any purchase, or have No Ads or VIP, do not see interstitial ads. Ads are served through the AppLovin MAX mediation platform, which runs a real-time auction between several ad networks (listed below). To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these companies may collect your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, only where allowed), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system, language, app version, and how you interact with ads.

Analytics and crash reports. To understand how players progress, balance the game and fix errors, we use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config with A/B Testing, and GameAnalytics. They receive an installation identifier, the advertising identifier where you allowed it, device model, operating system, language, country (derived from IP address), game events such as levels reached and items used, purchase events without payment details, ad revenue per impression, and crash logs. Remote Config may give different players slightly different settings, for example ad frequency or offer prices, so we can compare them.

Install attribution. We use Tenjin to learn which advertising campaign, if any, led to an install, and whether our own marketing pays off. Tenjin receives the advertising identifier where you allowed it, IP address, device information, install and purchase events, and ad revenue data.

Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms. The game receives only a confirmation of which item was bought, so it can deliver it to you and restore it later. We do not receive your payment card or billing address.

Notifications. Reminders such as "your daily chest is ready" are scheduled on your device. We do not run a push server and do not collect a push token. You can turn them off in the game or in your phone's settings.

Support emails. If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to answer you and keep the conversation for up to two years.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Until you answer the consent form, advertising and analytics storage are switched off. We rely on your consent for personalised advertising, for access to your device's advertising identifier, for analytics (Firebase Analytics and GameAnalytics) and for install attribution (Tenjin), and on our legitimate interest in running and securing the game for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and crash reports. You can withdraw consent at any time (see below).

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it loads no third-party fonts or scripts. It stores nothing in your browser. Like any web server, our hosting keeps short technical logs (IP address, browser type, page requested and time) to keep the site running and secure. These logs are deleted automatically after a limited period.

Your choices and rights

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the game shows a consent form on first launch. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings (Privacy settings).
  • Tracking on iPhone: if you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the game cannot access your IDFA. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android: go to Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads) to reset or delete it. Ads will then not be personalised.
  • Game data: Settings › Delete data in the game, or uninstalling the game, removes all progress stored on your device. To also delete analytics data linked to your device, write to us (see the next point).
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may ask what personal data we or our providers hold about you, and ask to correct, delete or stop processing it. Write to info@protekgames.com. Because we have no accounts, include the game name and, if you can, your device's advertising ID so we can find related data. We reply within 30 days.
  • We do not sell personal information for money. Some laws treat sharing data with ad networks as a "sale" or "sharing"; you can opt out by declining consent or limiting ad tracking as described above.
  • You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our games are intended for players aged 13 and over and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has used our game and data about them was collected, contact us and we will help delete it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
